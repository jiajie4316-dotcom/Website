Our Philosophy

A passion for honest food & memorable evenings

Founded in 2014, Aura was conceived as an intimate escape where refined culinary techniques meet the rustic warmth of wood-fired ovens and fresh botanical cocktails.

Every dish is crafted from locally grown produce and sustainably harvested seafood, reflecting our dedication to ethical gastronomy.
